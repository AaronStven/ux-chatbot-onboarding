# Usability Study Protocol: Chatbot Onboarding

## 1. Objective
Evaluate the effectiveness and user satisfaction of three different onboarding flows for a university campus chatbot (e.g., "Ask Uni").

## 2. Participants
- **N = 12** university students (mixed years and faculties).
- Screened to ensure a mix of tech-savvy and non-tech-savvy users.

## 3. Methodology (Within-Subjects Design)
Participants interact with all three prototypes (A: Text-heavy, B: Carousel/Buttons, C: Conversational AI).
- **Task:** "Ask the chatbot how to reset your student password and then ask it for the library's weekend hours."
- **Think-Aloud Protocol:** Participants speak their thoughts aloud while completing tasks.
- **Metrics Collected:**
  - Task Completion Rate (%)
  - Time on Task (seconds)
  - Error Rate (misclicks, dead ends)
  - System Usability Scale (SUS) - Post-test questionnaire
  - NASA-TLX - Mental workload

## 4. Procedure
1. Welcome & Consent (5 mins)
2. Pre-test interview (5 mins)
3. Task execution on Prototype A, B, and C (15 mins)
4. Post-test SUS & NASA-TLX surveys (5 mins)
5. Semi-structured debrief interview (10 mins)
